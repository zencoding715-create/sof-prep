Army Psychological Operations (PSYOP)

Army accession or in-service transfer

Entry route & prerequisites

New-soldier public requirements include U.S. citizenship, DLAB 85+, passing Army Fitness Test and waist-to-height standards, Airborne qualification and secret-clearance eligibility. Current-soldier requirements differ; consult official page.

Enlisted and officer routes

**Enlisted:** The Army lists new-soldier and current-Soldier routes with different citizenship, DLAB/AFT, GT, service-time, Airborne and clearance rules. Check the current PSYOP page for the route you qualify for.

**Officer:** Officers may access Civil Affairs/PSYOP roles through branch or functional-area assignments and in-service selection; this is not the same as the enlisted PSYOP accession path. Verify current branch, rank and service prerequisites with Army Special Operations recruiting.

Fitness preparation

Prepare current Army fitness events and field durability. No role-specific public numeric entrant percentile is available.

Published fitness events / targets

No current, complete public numeric cutoff table is included here for this pathway. Confirm the exact assessment, scoring, and test conditions directly with the official recruiter. This guide deliberately does not infer pass thresholds or pipeline percentiles.

Official references

Use service sources for the current requirements and test protocol. Requirements may change, and eligibility depends on your service status and entry route.

How to use this guide

These pages summarize publicly available career and fitness information. A published screening minimum is not a guarantee of selection and should not be treated as a prediction. Confirm route-specific medical, academic, administrative, and physical standards with an authorized recruiter. Underwater breath-hold events must only be performed as part of qualified, supervised testing; never self-test or train underwater alone.
