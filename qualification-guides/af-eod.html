Air Force Explosive Ordnance Disposal (EOD)

Air Force EOD IFT and technical training pipeline

Entry route & prerequisites

Confirm current accession route, ASVAB, citizenship/clearance, medical and specialty qualifications with Air Force recruiting. EOD is a technical combat-support career and has its own IFT variant.

Enlisted and officer routes

Enlisted: Air Force EOD is an enlisted specialty with an EOD-specific IFT component and technical training pipeline.

Officer: EOD is not listed as a standard direct-entry commissioned officer AFSC. Officers may lead EOD units through separate career assignments; verify current options with an Air Force recruiter.

Fitness preparation

The official Air Force IFT worksheet states the test is for Special Warfare, EOD or SERE and candidates must pass each role-specific component in one uninterrupted evaluation. Confirm current EOD event targets with recruiter.

Published fitness events / targets

No current, complete public numeric cutoff table is included here for this pathway. Confirm the exact assessment, scoring, and test conditions directly with the official recruiter. This guide deliberately does not infer pass thresholds or pipeline percentiles.

Official references

Use service sources for the current requirements and test protocol. Requirements may change, and eligibility depends on your service status and entry route.
