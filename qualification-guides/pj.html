Air Force Pararescue (PJ)

Air Force Special Warfare IFT and PJ pipeline

Entry route & prerequisites

Confirm current enlisted/officer route, ASVAB, citizenship/age, medical and specialty qualifications with Air Force Special Warfare recruiting.

Enlisted and officer routes

Enlisted: PJ is an enlisted Air Force Special Warfare specialty; applicants enter through the enlisted candidate route and pass the applicable IFT and training pipeline.

Officer: The officer counterpart for combat rescue is Combat Rescue Officer (CRO), with a separate commissioning route, officer selection process and IFT. It is not an officer PJ contract.

Fitness preparation

Candidate-course IFT public targets shown by AETC: 500 m swim ≤15:00; two 25 m underwater events pass; 1.5-mile run ≤10:20; 8 pull-ups, 50 sit-ups, 40 push-ups. Underwater events only in a qualified supervised test.
