Air Force Combat Control (CCT)

Air Force Special Warfare IFT and CCT pipeline

Entry route & prerequisites

Confirm current enlisted/officer route, aptitude, ASVAB and medical qualifications with Air Force Special Warfare recruiting. Specialty requirements may differ from shared candidate-course IFT.

Enlisted and officer routes

**Enlisted:** CCT is an enlisted specialty with its own candidate route, IFT and pipeline.

**Officer:** The related officer specialty is Special Tactics Officer (STO), accessed through a separate officer selection and training route. IFT requirements overlap in some events but the officer path and responsibilities differ.

Fitness preparation

Candidate-course IFT public targets: 500 m swim ≤15:00; two 25 m underwater events pass; 1.5-mile run ≤10:20; 8 pull-ups, 50 sit-ups, 40 push-ups. Underwater events only in qualified supervised testing.
