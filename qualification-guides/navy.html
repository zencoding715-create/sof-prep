Navy SEAL

Navy PST / elevated PST (EPST)

Entry route & prerequisites

Navy accession requirements include age, citizenship and clearance eligibility, medical/dive physical, vision and ASVAB criteria. Confirm current details and contract route with a Navy special warfare recruiter.

Enlisted and officer routes

Enlisted: Enter through the Navy SEAL accession contract when eligible, then complete PST, medical screening and the SEAL training pipeline.

Officer: SEAL officer applicants generally commission via an eligible Navy officer source (for example, Academy, NROTC or OCS), meet officer-specific age/degree/medical rules and compete through the officer selection process. Officer screening and career timing differ from enlisted contracts.

Fitness preparation

The Navy publishes PST minimum and elevated targets. These are screening benchmarks, not selection odds. Underwater work must only be assessed under qualified supervision.

How to use this guide

These pages summarize publicly available career and fitness information. A published screening minimum is not a guarantee of selection and should not be treated as a prediction. Confirm route-specific medical, academic, administrative, and physical standards with an authorized recruiter.
