Navy Explosive Ordnance Disposal (EOD)

Navy EOD PST / elevated PST (EPST)

Entry route & prerequisites

Official Navy page lists age, citizenship/clearance eligibility, eyesight, ASVAB and separate diver medical screening requirements; officer and in-service routes differ. Verify current requirements.

Enlisted and officer routes

Enlisted: Enlisted EOD candidates use the EOD Warrior Challenge/PST and enlisted training route, with ASVAB, age, vision and diver medical requirements.

Officer: EOD officer opportunities follow a separate commissioned-officer accession or in-service designator path and have distinct education, selection and duty requirements. Ask an EOD officer recruiter whether a current direct-accession route is available.

Fitness preparation

Navy EOD has a distinct published PST. Current Navy.com page lists 500 yd combat sidestroke 12:30 minimum/10:30 elevated; push-ups and sit-ups 50/70; pull-ups 6/10; 1.5-mile run 12:30/10:00. Verify current protocol.

How to use this guide

These pages summarize publicly available career and fitness information. A published screening minimum is not a guarantee of selection and should not be treated as a prediction. Confirm route-specific medical, academic, administrative, and physical standards with an authorized recruiter. Underwater breath-hold events must only be performed as part of qualified, supervised testing; never self-test or train underwater alone.
