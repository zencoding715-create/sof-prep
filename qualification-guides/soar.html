160th SOAR / Night Stalkers

Qualified aviation career pathway

Entry route & prerequisites

This is a specialized aviation regiment. Official Army overview describes qualifying MOS, clearance eligibility and Airborne qualification; it is not a general direct-entry fitness contract.

Enlisted and officer routes

Enlisted: Serve in a qualifying aviation MOS and meet 160th SOAR recruiting, clearance, Airborne and unit requirements. This is generally a prior-service/qualified-Soldier path.

Officer: Officers generally commission and qualify in Army aviation first, then apply through the 160th’s officer recruiting process. Aviation qualification and current unit needs govern eligibility.

Fitness preparation

Maintain branch fitness and role-specific aviation/medical qualifications. Confirm qualifying MOS/rating and selection steps with the 160th recruiting team.

Published fitness events / targets

No current, complete public numeric cutoff table is included here for this pathway. Confirm the exact assessment, scoring, and test conditions directly with the official recruiter. This guide deliberately does not infer pass thresholds or pipeline percentiles.

Official references

Use service sources for the current requirements and test protocol. Requirements may change, and eligibility depends on your service status and entry route.
