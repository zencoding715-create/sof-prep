75th Ranger Regiment

Pre-RASP screening and RASP pathway

Entry route & prerequisites

Qualifying Army accession or in-service route; eligibility, MOS, airborne and current screening rules depend on status.

Enlisted and officer routes

Enlisted: Join or serve in an eligible Army MOS, meet accession and Airborne requirements, then compete for RASP through the route available to your status.

Officer: Commission and branch into an eligible role, then apply/compete for the officer Ranger pathway (RASP 2); officer assignment and eligibility differ from enlisted RASP.

Fitness preparation

Published public events include pre-RASP hand-release push-ups, plank, pull-ups and 2-mile run. GoArmy describes a 5-mile run under 40:00, 15 m uniform swim and 12-mile march with 35 lb as RASP success goals. Verify current protocol with recruiting.
