Coast Guard Aviation Survival Technician (AST) · Rescue Swimmer

AST A Prep then AST A School physical evaluation

Entry route & prerequisites

Coast Guard accession and AST training access apply. Current Coast Guard handbook says AST A Prep must be completed before A School. Confirm accession, medical and class availability with recruiter.

Enlisted and officer routes

**Enlisted:** AST is an enlisted Coast Guard rating. Applicants follow Coast Guard accession and rating eligibility, then complete AST Prep and AST A School requirements.

**Officer:** AST rescue swimmer is an enlisted rating, not a direct commissioned-officer specialty. Coast Guard officers may serve in aviation or rescue leadership roles through separate commissioning and flight training paths.

Fitness preparation

The official handbook lists a timed physical evaluation including rows, push-ups, side plank, pull-ups, shuttle, gear swim, gear sprints and underwater sequences. Exact protocol is presented in the official handbook; the underwater sequences are supervised course assessments only.
