Army Civil Affairs (CA)

Army accession or in-service qualification

Entry route & prerequisites

Route and component matter. Official guidance includes clearance eligibility, Airborne qualification and the Civil Affairs qualification course; see current requirements for enlisted/officer and serving routes.

Enlisted and officer routes

Enlisted: Enlisted Soldiers use the enlisted Civil Affairs qualification path; component, MOS, clearance, Airborne and service requirements vary.

Officer: Civil Affairs Officer opportunities use officer branch/functional-area and in-service selection routes, with separate rank, service and qualification requirements. Confirm current policy with CA recruiting.

Fitness preparation

Build general Army fitness, field endurance, communication and cultural learning. No role-specific public fitness cutoff is listed here.

Published fitness events / targets

No current, complete public numeric cutoff table is included here for this pathway. Confirm the exact assessment, scoring, and test conditions directly with the official recruiter. This guide deliberately does not infer pass thresholds or pipeline percentiles.

Official references

Use service sources for the current requirements and test protocol. Requirements may change, and eligibility depends on your service status and entry route.

How to use this guide

These pages summarize publicly available career and fitness information. A published screening minimum is not a guarantee of selection and should not be treated as a prediction. Confirm route-specific medical, academic, administrative, and physical standards with an authorized recruiter.
