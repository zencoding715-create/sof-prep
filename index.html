Standards are pathway-specific

Some services publish event-by-event screening minimums; others publish event lists or separate eligibility rules. This tool only compares numeric events where we can cite a current official source. The Navy SEAL and AIRR values below are published PST minimum and elevated targets; Air Force IFT and Ranger screening fields use published candidate-entry targets; Coast Guard AST events come from its current training handbook. For pathways without comparable public score tables, confirm current testing and entry rules with an official recruiter.

How “who tends to do best” is handled: Selection outcomes depend on many factors and the services do not publish a universal candidate ranking model. This guide highlights preparation traits supported by the demands of the pathway—event-specific fitness, swimming comfort where relevant, sustained work capacity, learning, teamwork, and eligibility readiness. It does not predict selection odds.

Train progressively. Do not train through pain or ignore medical restrictions. Never practice underwater breath-holding or hypoxic drills alone. Stop for chest pain, faintness, unusual shortness of breath, or severe pain; consult a qualified clinician before beginning if you have a health concern. Your recruiter is the authority for current eligibility, test protocols, and scheduling.
