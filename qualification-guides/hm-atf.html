Navy Hospital Corpsman ATF / SOIDC

HM-ATF / SOIDC screening and medical training route

Entry route & prerequisites

Confirm current HM-ATF/SOIDC contract, citizenship/clearance, medical and PST requirements with Navy recruiting. This program-specific route is distinct from general Hospital Corpsman accession.

Enlisted and officer routes

Enlisted: HM-ATF/SOIDC is an enlisted Hospital Corpsman advanced technical field; contract, screening and medical training requirements must be confirmed with Navy recruiting.

Officer: There is no direct commissioned-officer SOIDC/HM-ATF accession matching this enlisted pipeline. Navy medical officers follow separate professional commissioning and qualification routes.

Fitness preparation

Build swimming, running, calisthenics and medical-learning readiness. Use only current HM-ATF standards supplied by an official recruiter; avoid substituting SEAL thresholds.
