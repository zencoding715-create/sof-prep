Air Force SERE Specialist

Air Force SERE IFT and specialist pipeline

Entry route & prerequisites

Official Air Force career page lists U.S. citizenship, age 17–41 at enlistment, ASVAB General 55+, TAPAS 55+, reading and clearance eligibility. Confirm all current eligibility and route details with recruiter.

Enlisted and officer routes

Enlisted: SERE Specialist is an enlisted Air Force specialty with an enlisted IFT and specialist training pipeline.

Officer: SERE Specialist is not a direct-entry commissioned officer career field. Officers may lead units or serve in related personnel recovery roles through separate officer career paths.

Fitness preparation

Official public SERE prep targets: 8 pull-ups, 48 sit-ups in 2 min, 40 push-ups in 2 min, 1.5-mile run under 11:00, and water confidence. The exact current IFT protocol should be recruiter-confirmed.
