Navy Diver

Navy Diver PST / dive medical screening

Entry route & prerequisites

Confirm current enlisted/officer route, eyesight/ASVAB, dive physical and medical qualification with a Navy recruiter and Diving Medical Officer.

Enlisted and officer routes

Enlisted: Navy Diver is an enlisted rating with a Diver-specific PST, dive medical screening and diver training route.

Officer: The Navy lists Diver opportunities for officers as well; officer eligibility and assignment depend on designator and service needs and require separate diving medical/screening approval. Confirm route with a Navy diving recruiter.

Fitness preparation

Navy publishes Diver-specific PST/EPST values. Confirm the latest event-by-event minimums on the official career page; do not use another Navy community’s numbers. Never practice breath-holding alone.

How to use this guide

These pages summarize publicly available career and fitness information. A published screening minimum is not a guarantee of selection and should not be treated as a prediction. Confirm route-specific medical, academic, administrative, and physical standards with an authorized recruiter. Underwater breath-hold events must only be performed as part of qualified, supervised testing; never self-test or train underwater alone.
