Army Special Forces / Green Beret

SFAS pathway; 18X or in-service routes

Entry route & prerequisites

Army SOF route, citizenship/age, ASVAB, clearance, airborne and SFAS eligibility depend on accession status. Use the current official SFAS Preparation Handbook and recruiter guidance.

Enlisted and officer routes

Enlisted: Eligible applicants may pursue the 18X Special Forces Candidate route, then complete initial Army training, Airborne and SFAS-related steps. Prior-service enlisted Soldiers use their current-service eligibility route.

Officer: Officers normally serve first, meet branch and career prerequisites, then apply for SFAS through the officer route; 18X is an enlisted accession option, not an officer contract.

Fitness preparation

Build durable running, strength, load carriage and self-management; no single public fitness score predicts selection.

Published fitness events / targets

No current, complete public numeric cutoff table is included here for this pathway. Confirm the exact assessment, scoring, and test conditions directly with the official recruiter. This guide deliberately does not infer pass thresholds or pipeline percentiles.

Official references

Use service sources for the current requirements and test protocol. Requirements may change, and eligibility depends on your service status and entry route.

How to use this guide

These pages summarize publicly available career and fitness information. A published screening minimum is not a guarantee of selection and should not be treated as a prediction. Confirm route-specific medical, academic, administrative, and physical standards with an authorized recruiter.
