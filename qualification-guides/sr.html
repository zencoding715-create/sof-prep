Air Force Special Reconnaissance (SR)

Air Force Special Warfare IFT and SR pipeline

Entry route & prerequisites

Confirm current enlisted/officer route, aptitude, ASVAB, medical and clearance qualifications with Air Force Special Warfare recruiting.

Enlisted and officer routes

Enlisted: Special Reconnaissance is an enlisted specialty with an enlisted candidate path and role-specific IFT/pipeline.

Officer: There is no one-for-one public direct-entry “SR Officer” path on the Air Force career listing. Officers may serve in related Special Tactics roles after separate selection; ask an Air Force Special Warfare recruiter about current options.

Fitness preparation

AETC candidate-course IFT includes swim, underwater events, run and calisthenics. Confirm current SR-specific test protocol with recruiter; underwater events only in qualified supervision.

Published fitness events / targets

No current, complete public numeric cutoff table is included here for this pathway. Confirm the exact assessment, scoring, and test conditions directly with the official recruiter. This guide deliberately does not infer pass thresholds or pipeline percentiles.

Official references

Use service sources for the current requirements and test protocol. Requirements may change, and eligibility depends on your service status and entry route.

How to use this guide

These pages summarize publicly available career and fitness information. A published screening minimum is not a guarantee of selection and should not be treated as a prediction. Confirm route-specific medical, academic, administrative, and physical standards with an authorized recruiter. Underwater breath-hold events must only be performed as part of qualified, supervised testing; never self-test or train underwater alone.
